# Reading and video

 Forty years of people have documented how they did it, and all of it is free. Start with the video.

{#watch}
 01 Watch

  Video · Veritasium The Fastest Maze-Solving Competition On Earth Watch this one first. Search runs, speed runs and why top mice look the way they do.  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMQbHMgK2rw>  Video · UKMARS A full contest run, start to finish What an entire slot looks like: explore, return, speed run, escalate.  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Itos2Z6orQ>  Video · IEEE Bruins Micromouse lecture: flood fill A whiteboard-level university lecture, exactly the depth you need.  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktn3C7aXVR0>  Video · APEC 2024 HAL900's fastest run yet Top-tier diagonal running. Watch the route it chooses, not just the speed.  <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNYlBb1Paxo>

{#rules-reading}
 02 Rules and books

  Rules · ukmars.org UK Micromouse Classic rules The definitive statement of the standard we run to.  <https://ukmars.org/contests/contest-rules/micromouse-classic/>  Book · micromouseonline.com Peter Harrison's Micromouse book The best single resource in the sport: chassis, sensors, motion control and maze solving.  <https://micromouseonline.com/micromouse-book/>  Chapter · micromouseonline.com Mazes and maze solving Flood fill, exploration and route cost, written for micromouse.  <https://micromouseonline.com/micromouse-book/mazes-and-maze-solving/>  Overview · Wikipedia Micromouse A fast orientation to the sport, its variants and its records.  <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Micromouse>

 03 Code to build on

  GitHub · mackorone/mms The micromouse simulator Test your algorithm with no robot. Turn up with a working flood fill.  <https://github.com/mackorone/mms>  GitHub · ukmars mazerunner-core Complete, readable micromouse firmware: motion profiles, sensors, flood fill and search.  <https://github.com/ukmars/mazerunner-core>  GitHub · micromouseonline mazefiles Decades of real contest mazes to test your solver against.  <https://github.com/micromouseonline/mazefiles>
